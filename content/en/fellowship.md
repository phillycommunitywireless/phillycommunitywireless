A program of Philly Community Wireless

## About the Fellowship

Philly Community Wireless (PCW) operates free public Wi-Fi across Norris Square, Fairhill, and Kensington: a community-controlled network with hundreds of access points. The Public Internet Fellowship invites local artists, technologists, and residents to use our network as a platform for community media, supporting new ways of empowering communities to come together in shared spaces to use the internet to better their lives and spaces.

## How to apply

The call for proposals is coming later this year. In the meantime, sponsors and interested applicants with inquiries about eligibility can reach us through our [contact page](/contact).

## Funders

Funded support for the Public Internet Fellowship pilot is provided by People's Media Fund through its Future Fund. Additional funding from the William Penn Foundation and the Claneil Foundation has made this initiative possible.
