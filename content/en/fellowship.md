## About the Fellowship

Philly Community Wireless (PCW) operates free public Wi-Fi across Norris Square, Fairhill, and Kensington: a community-controlled network with hundreds of access points. The Public Internet Fellowship invites local artists, technologists, and residents to use our network as a platform for community media, supporting new ways of empowering communities to come together in shared spaces to use the internet to better their lives and spaces.

PCW received $75,000 from the People's Media Fund to pay local artists and residents to build things on the network. Over the pilot year, three to five fellows will each be hosted by a neighborhood organization that stewards a public space, and will create and install interactive art, environmental resources, and new forms of community digital storytelling that bring people together in a shared space and give voice to their diverse perspectives through access to the internet.

Practical projects might be in the vein of things like bus arrival or air monitor displays. Creative projects might be light sculptures that move with the weather, or stories using augmented reality on people's smartphones.

## Advisory Board

The Fellowship is guided by an Advisory Board of community organizations that steward public spaces in Norris Square, Fairhill, and Kensington. The board shapes the open call and the review criteria, reads the applications and chooses the fellows, and hosts the fellows' installations and events, so that the people who steward these spaces decide what gets built in them.

- Kensington Corridor Trust
- Norris Square Neighborhood Project
- New Kensington CDC
- HACE
- César Andreu Iglesias Community Garden
- Historic Fair Hill
- Taller Puertorriqueño

Additional organizations are joining the board this fall.

## The pilot year

The board met for the first time in September 2026 and is finalizing the open call, which publishes this fall. Fellows will be chosen over the winter and announced in March 2027. Projects are developed and installed from April through July, and the year ends with showcase events at the host sites in August 2027.

## Get involved

The call for proposals is coming later this year. In the meantime, interested applicants with inquiries about eligibility can reach us through our [contact page](/contact).

We welcome sponsors to reach out with interest. Additional support goes directly to more fellows, larger stipends, and deeper technical support for the projects.
