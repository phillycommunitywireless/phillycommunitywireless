Alex is a co-founder of PCW. As a long-term volunteer and, more recently, Executive Director, Alex's work and knowledge base spans most aspects of the organization, including but not limited to building assessments and installs, other technical work, operations, partnership building, and leading events. A resident of North Philadelphia, Alex was named Technical.ly's Tech Community Leader of the Year in 2022.
