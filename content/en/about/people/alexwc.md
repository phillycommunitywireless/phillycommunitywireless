Alex Wermer-Colan is a co-founder, long-time volunteer, and Executive Director of Philly Community Wireless. He holds a PhD in English from the CUNY Graduate Center and works as the Academic and Research Director at Temple University Libraries' Loretta C. Duckworth Scholars Studio. A resident of North Philadelphia, Alex was named Technical.ly's Tech Community Leader of the Year in 2022.
