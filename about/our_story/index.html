Our Story

Founded in 2020 during the COVID-19 pandemic, Philly Community Wireless started as a volunteer-led project made up of librarians and teachers, organizers, technologists, and community leaders.

For an interactive map of our history, please view our Scrollytelling map!

Our History

A timeline of Philly Community Wireless activities, milestones, and accomplishments.
  1. 2020 July 2020

    PCW Begins

    The very beginning! Our first node was installed by partner PhillyWisper in July of 2020. PCW officially started building at Norris Square Neighborhood Project in the spring of 2021, mostly led by PhillyWisper. Starting late summer 2021, PCW volunteers began leading installs.
  2. 2021 October 2021

    PCW Install

    Install done at a resident’s home near Norris Square Park. By December 2021, we had installed 8 Access Points throughout Norris Square.
  3. December 2021

    PCW Establishes HQ at NSNP

    PCW moves into the basement of the Norris Square Neighborhood Project, strengthening our partnership with NSNP. Having our headquarters at this location proves to be perfect for our ongoing work in building out our network and partnerships in the area.
  4. 2022 March 2022

    Big Picture Alliance Documentary

    PCW’s first documentary filmed and produced by Big Picture Alliance! See it at PCW’s YouTube channel.
  5. April 2022

    First Solar Powered Install

    Holobiont Lab’s Solar Mesh Node (a self-contained solar powered set-up) used to power a Wi-Fi access point at NSNP’s Villa Africana Colobó Garden.
  6. April 2022

    First Purple Air Monitor Installed

    PCW installs a PurpleAir Monitor (used to monitor air quality) at Las Parcelas Gardens. Air monitors are an example of the Internet of Things devices that public Wi-Fi, such as ours, can facilitate.
  7. May 2022

    Install at The Clay Studio

    Our first major install on roof of The Clay Studio.
  8. July 2022

    Install for Iglesias Gardens

    A nearby resident hosts some of our network infrastructure equipment to expand Wi-Fi into Iglesias Gardens.
  9. September 2022

    Wi-Fi Town Hall

    PCW’s first in-person Wi-Fi Town Hall hosted at Iglesias Gardens!
  10. November 2022

    Tech Team Meeting

    PCW volunteer-based Tech Team meeting in West Philly, configuring access points and doing data work.
  11. 2023 February 2023

    PCW Presents at Iffy Books

    PCW volunteers present at Iffy Books about the network we’ve built so far, where we’re planning to expand, and what we’ve learned along they way. We also debuted our network coverage map!
  12. April 2023

    PCW Headquarters Renovation

    Updates done at PCW Headquarters in Norris Square Neighborhood Project building.
  13. May 2023

    Digital Literacy Alliance Presentation

    An overview presentation of Philly Community Wireless with the City of Philadelphia’s Digital Literacy Alliance.
  14. May 2023

    Big Picture Alliance and PCW Presentation

    PCW and Big Picture Alliance showcase our documentary and host a Q&A at The Holodec.
  15. June 2023

    Kensington Mutual Aid Fair

    PCW volunteers table at the Kensington Free Library Mutual Aid Fair. PCW continues to table at various events throughout Philadelphia.
  16. December 2023

    Install at HACE Properties Begins

    The beginning of PCW’s first multi-dwelling residential building install. Together with PhillyWisper, we installed the main radio on HACE’s Casa Caribe and set up a PowerBeam to bring connectivity to HACE’s Caribe Towers.
  17. 2024 February 2024

    Indoor Install Work at HACE Property

    Running ethernet cable through HACE’s Caribe Towers to set up indoor access points for the residents and staff. PCW went on to provide Wi-Fi service in all common spaces on all floors of the building.
  18. March 2024

    PC Distribution

    Laptop distribution with PCs for People to give laptops out to HACE residents at Casa Caribe, Caribe Towers, and the Villas del Caribe. In addition to the laptops, PCW provided technical support to residents who required help setting up their new laptops.
  19. May 2024

    Install at Taller Puertorriqueño

    PCW constantly expands and improves our network by revisiting sites. Here, we returned to Taller Puertorriqueño to provide maintenance to existing infrastructure and add another AP facing the street.
  20. May 2024

    Volunteer Session

    The team at Holobiont Lab provides a walk-through and troubleshooting training on Solar Mesh Nodes.
  21. June 2024

    Install at NKCDC

    PCW installs a new Wi-Fi hub with PhillyWisper at NKCDC alongside our volunteers and interns, helping to build out the network to cover a large perimeter around Kensington Ave and Somerset.
  22. August 2024

    Install for NKCDC's Tusculum Garden

    With the support of a local resident, and using a relay from NKCDC’s building, we set up connectivity for Tusculum Gateway Garden along Kensington Ave.
  23. September 2024

    Outreach at Jose Manual Collazo Playground

    PCW staff and volunteers tabling at Jose Manual Collazo Playground with Team NAS alongside other community organizations.
  24. September 2024

    Work with Bryn Mawr Students

    Environmental Studies students from Bryn Mawr College come out to PCW headquarters to learn more about our organization. Collaboration throughout their semester led to three distinct presentations incorporating elements of PCW’s work and future goals, including air monitoring.
  25. November 2024

    Donation from Community Internet Solutions

    PCW receives a massive donation from Pittsburgh’s Community Internet Solutions (formerly known as Meta Mesh) as they close up shop.
  26. December 2024

    PCW Keeps Growing

    4 and a half years after starting, PCW has grown and expanded throughout the neighborhoods of Norris Square, Fairhill, and Kensington, servicing tens of thousands of users. To see our live service map, visit our network map.
